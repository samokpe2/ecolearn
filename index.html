About Eco-learn

Leading the Charge for Climate Literacy, thereby Transforming Knowledge into Climate Action

ECO-LEARN Nigeria is a groundbreaking climate education project aimed at raising awareness and fostering action on climate change in Lagos State.

At Eco-Learn Nigeria, our mission is to empower and equip communities in Nigeria with the knowledge, skills, and resources necessary to effectively drive climate action. By empowering our communities, we ensure that we have a prepared generation to tackle environmental challenges and build a more sustainable future.

Why Eco-Learn

Building a Sustainable Future Through Climate Education 

At Eco-Learn Nigeria, we believe that education is the foundation for meaningful climate action. By equipping individuals especially teachers and students with the right knowledge, skills, and resources, we are shaping a generation prepared to tackle climate challenges and drive sustainable solutions.  

Promoting Economic Stability & Green Opportunities

Through climate literacy, we prepare youth and communities for a green economy, fostering long-term economic resilience. 

Strengthening Climate Resilience & Global Security 

By raising awareness and promoting climate action at all levels, we help reduce risks, enhance security, and support a more sustainable and peaceful future.  

Safeguarding Communities and Environments

With climate education communities are empowered to change, adapt, build resilience to thrive for a sustainable future.



Activities

Equipping Our Communities for Climate Action

Through carefully designed workshops, hands-on training, and readily accessible resources, we support educators in bringing climate topics into the classroom. Our activities empower teachers with the practical skills they need to build awareness, drive action, and inspire students toward sustainable living.

Events

Paving the Way for Sustainable Change

The ECO-LEARN Nigeria project launched officially May 25th 2024. Sponsored by the Netherlands Consulate General in Lagos, the planning and organization were managed by Earthplus Sustainability Network, YSDN, and WhatisDesign, and amplified by the Dutch Youth Advisory Committee in collaboration with established institutions.
