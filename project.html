ECO-LEARN PROJECT LAUNCH

    May 28th, 2024    Lagos, Nigeria

The ECO-LEARN Nigeria project launched officially May 25th 2024. Sponsored by the Netherlands Consulate General in Lagos, the planning and organization were managed by Earthplus Sustainability Network, YSDN, and WhatisDesign, and amplified by the Dutch Youth Advisory Committee in collaboration with established institutions. The initiative also saw support from the Lagos State Public Service Staff Development Centre and the Ministry of Environment.

The launch event was held at the Public Service Staff Development Centre in the Magodo area of Lagos, starting at 10 AM. It was attended by the Deputy Consular of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in Lagos, The Head of Department Office of climate cahnge and sustainable development, AG Director General PSSDC and other notable stakeholders. Teachers from the six district schools in Lagos State were present, and the event saw great attendance and attention from media houses such as TVC and LTV.

85 people were in attendance. With selected participating teachers and all other stakeholders.
