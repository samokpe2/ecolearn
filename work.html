Our Work

Activities

Equipping Educators and Students for Climate Leadership

Through carefully designed workshops, hands-on training, and readily accessible resources, we support educators in bringing climate topics into the classroom. Our activities empower teachers with the practical skills they need to build awareness, drive action, and inspire students toward sustainable living.



Events

Paving the Way for Sustainable Change

The ECO-LEARN Nigeria project launched officially May 25th 2024. Sponsored by the Netherlands Consulate General in Lagos, the planning and organization were managed by Earthplus Sustainability Network, YSDN, and WhatisDesign, and amplified by the Dutch Youth Advisory Committee in collaboration with established institutions.
