About Us

Unifying for Climate Action Through Learning

The heart of Lagos pulsed with a different kind of energy on May 31st, 2023. Freedom Park wasn't just a historic landmark that day; it became a platform for over twenty youth organizations to unite. Their message, a powerful chorus that resonated across the city, was clear: climate action starts with education. This wasn't just a gathering; it was the sowing of a seed, a seed of change that would take root and blossom in a most unexpected way.

Fast forward a year, and that seed has sprouted into a remarkable initiative – ECO-LEARN Nigeria. This program isn't just another top-down solution dreamed up in distant offices. It's the brainchild of a unique collaboration, a testament to the power of young people at the helm of decision-making. The Netherlands Consulate, recognizing the potential ignited at Freedom Park, played a crucial role in bringing together a diverse group of stakeholders.

Leading the charge were youth organizations like the Youth Sustainable Development Network, What is Design, and the Earth Sustainability Network. Their voices, amplified by the Dutch Youth Advisory Committee, resonated with established institutions. The Lagos State Public Service Servants Development Centre and the Ministry of Environment joined forces, recognizing the importance of youth leadership in tackling a challenge that will define the future.

Vision

Universal Access to Climate Education by 2030.

At Eco-Learn Nigeria, we envision a future where climate education is accessible to all—children, youth, and adults—whether directly or indirectly. We firmly believe that widespread climate literacy is the foundation for a sustainable and resilient future, empowering individuals and communities to take meaningful action against climate challenges.

Mission

Empowering Educators, Inspiring Change.

At Eco-Learn Nigeria, our mission is to equip public school teachers in Nigeria with the knowledge, skills, and resources necessary to effectively educate and inspire young minds on climate change. By empowering educators, we ensure that climate literacy spreads through classrooms and communities, fostering a generation prepared to tackle environmental challenges and build a more sustainable future.

Goals

Driving Sustainable Impact:
Goals for 2025

01

Integrating Climate Education into School Curriculum

Advocate for and support the incorporation of climate education into national and local school curricula, ensuring that students gain essential knowledge and skills to address climate challenges.

02

Expanding Climate Awareness & Advocacy at All Levels

Foster climate literacy and proactive engagement across different demographics—students, educators, policymakers, and the general public—through campaigns, workshops, and multi-stakeholder collaborations.

03

Advancing Climate Action for Mitigation & Project Implementation

Implement practical climate solutions and energy solutions to mitigate the effects of climate change, focusing on community engagement, reforestation, waste reduction, and sustainable practices.



Team Members

A Collective of Changemakers.

Our team is a dedicated group of young leaders, educators, and experts working together to create a sustainable future. United by a shared passion for climate action, we bring diverse skills and perspectives to drive ECO-LEARN’s mission.
